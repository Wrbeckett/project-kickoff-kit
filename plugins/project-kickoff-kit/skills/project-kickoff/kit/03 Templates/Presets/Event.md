# Preset: Event

**Usual goal:** the event happens on <date> for <number> people, within <budget>, and <success measure: attendance, feedback score, sign-ups, money raised>.
**Usual setup level:** Solo for a small one-off; Standard with a team or sponsors; Team when several people organize.

## Suggested setup (confirm each)
- **Folders to add:** Venue, Guests and registration, Speakers or performers, Vendors, Marketing, Run sheet.
- **Sessions:** Lead (Opus, Medium) · Logistics (venue, vendors, run sheet; Sonnet, Medium) · Promotion (posts, emails, sign-ups; Sonnet, Medium).
- **Trackers:** Guests/registration; Vendors and bookings (Waits on: deposits, contracts, approvals).
- **Rhythm:** weekly brief until 4 weeks out, then a short daily brief; a final-week checklist; a follow-up and thank-you plan for the week after.
- **Usual rules:** no booking, paying or emailing guests without OK; every vendor has a confirmation date and a backup.

## Questions to add to the interview
- Date, venue status, expected numbers, budget and who approves spending.
- Who's speaking or performing; what each needs (travel, AV, accessibility).
- How people sign up; what reminders go out and when.
- What's the plan if it rains, a speaker cancels, or numbers double?

## Plugins that pair well (optional; suggest only if not installed and useful)
Marketing (promotion, posts, emails) · Small Business (if it's for a business: outreach, social content) · Productivity (task list and reminders).

## What could go wrong (start the check with these)
Venue or AV problems on the day · late speaker or vendor confirmations · low sign-ups · budget creep · no one owns the run sheet on the day.
