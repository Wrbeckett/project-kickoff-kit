# Preset: Website

**Usual goal:** a website live at <address> by <date> that <does what: brings inquiries, sells, informs>, measured by <visits, inquiries, sales, sign-ups>.
**Usual setup level:** Solo for a small site; Standard with content, design and build; Team when others write content or approve.

## Suggested setup (confirm each)
- **Folders to add:** Content (page text, by page), Design (mockups, brand), Website (deploy this folder), Images, Launch checklist.
- **Sessions:** Lead (Opus, Medium) · Build (Opus, Medium; Sonnet for small edits) · Content (page text, SEO basics; Sonnet, Medium).
- **Trackers:** Pages (status per page; Waits on: text, photos, approval).
- **Rhythm:** weekly build review; a pre-launch checklist (links, mobile, forms, speed, accessibility); a 30-day after-launch check.
- **Usual rules:** never deploy, change DNS or connect payments without OK; keep logins out of files; every page checked on a phone before launch.

## Questions to add to the interview
- Domain and hosting (already owned? where?), and who can log in.
- Pages needed; who writes and approves the text and photos.
- Forms, store, bookings or other features; any existing site to move.
- Brand: logo, colours, fonts, examples they like.

## Plugins that pair well (optional; suggest only if not installed and useful)
Small Business (SEO and AI visibility, brand style, content) · Design (mockups, critique, accessibility review) · Marketing (launch content).

## What could go wrong (start the check with these)
Content arrives late · domain or hosting access is missing · forms don't send · launch breaks old links · nobody owns updates after launch.
