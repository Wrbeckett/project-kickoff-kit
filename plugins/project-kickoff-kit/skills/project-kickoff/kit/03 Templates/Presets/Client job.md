# Preset: Client job

**Usual goal:** deliver <what> to <client> by <date> within <budget or fee>, approved by <client contact>, measured by <sign-off, results, repeat work>.
**Usual setup level:** Standard; Team if subcontractors or the client's staff work in the folder.

## Suggested setup (confirm each)
- **Folders to add:** Client brief, Proofs (dated rounds), Approvals, Invoices and quotes, Client assets.
- **Sessions:** Lead (Opus, Medium) · Production (the work itself; Opus, Medium) · Client communication (drafts of updates and emails for the owner to send; Sonnet, Medium).
- **Trackers:** Deliverables and rounds (status, round number, Waits on: client feedback, assets, payment).
- **Rhythm:** a short weekly client update draft; a proof-round log; a wrap-up and invoice check at the end.
- **Usual rules:** nothing goes to the client without the owner's OK; every round of changes is logged with the client's request; scope changes are flagged before work starts; client files stay private.

## Questions to add to the interview
- Scope: what's included, how many rounds of changes, what's extra.
- Client contact, who approves, how they like to receive proofs.
- Fee, payment terms, deadlines and any penalties.
- What the client already has (brand, files, accounts) and what they still owe.

## Plugins that pair well (optional; suggest only if not installed and useful)
Small Business (proposals, invoices, client follow-up) · Operations (status reports, change requests, risk assessment) · Design or Marketing, depending on the job.

## What could go wrong (start the check with these)
Scope creep · slow client feedback · unclear approval · assets or access missing · invoice or payment delays.
