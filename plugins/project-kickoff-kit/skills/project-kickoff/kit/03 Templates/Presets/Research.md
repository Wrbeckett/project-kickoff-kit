# Preset: Research

**Usual goal:** answer <question> by <date> as a <report, proposal, grant, review>, for <audience>, judged by <approval, acceptance, decision made>.
**Usual setup level:** Solo; Standard for a long project; Team with co-authors.

## Suggested setup (confirm each)
- **Folders to add:** Sources (with a source list), Notes, Data, Drafts, Submission.
- **Sessions:** Lead (Opus, Medium) · Research (finding and reading sources; Opus, High; Fable for very broad, deep research where the plan allows) · Writing (Opus, Medium; Sonnet for formatting and references).
- **Trackers:** Sources (read / to read / used, with the claim each supports); Sections (status; Waits on: data, a co-author, a source).
- **Rhythm:** weekly progress note; a mid-point "are we still answering the right question?" check; a final fact and citation check.
- **Usual rules:** every claim has a source; quotes are short and attributed; Claude's summaries are checked against the source before they go in the draft; ethics or data rules followed.

## Questions to add to the interview
- The exact question, who it's for and what decision it supports.
- Required format, length, citation style and deadline (and any submission system).
- Sources already in hand; databases or libraries they can reach.
- Co-authors, reviewers, ethics approval or data rules.

## Plugins that pair well (optional; suggest only if not installed and useful)
Data (analysis and charts) · Product Management (synthesizing research) · Operations (risk assessment for a feasibility study).

## What could go wrong (start the check with these)
The question drifts · sources can't be reached · a key claim has no source · writing starts too late · citation or format rules missed at submission.
