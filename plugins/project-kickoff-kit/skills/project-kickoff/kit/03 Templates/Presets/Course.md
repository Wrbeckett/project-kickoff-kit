# Preset: Course

**Usual goal:** a course or program ready by <date> that meets <outcomes or standards>, with <measure: completion, assessment results, student feedback, approval>.
**Usual setup level:** Solo for one instructor; Team when a program team or committee is involved.

## Suggested setup (confirm each)
- **Folders to add:** Course outline, Lessons (one folder per week or module), Assessments and rubrics, Student-facing, Approvals and policies.
- **Sessions:** Lead (Opus, Medium) · Course design (outcomes, outline, assessments; Opus, Medium) · Materials (handouts, slides, LMS text from approved outlines; Sonnet, Medium).
- **Trackers:** Lessons and materials (status per week; Waits on: outline approval, a source, a guest).
- **Rhythm:** weekly planning check before term; during term a short weekly "next week's materials" brief.
- **Usual rules:** institutional policies (academic integrity, accessibility, AI use) are checked on every student-facing item; no student names, grades or accommodations in project files unless policy allows.

## Questions to add to the interview
- Learning outcomes or program standards it must meet; who approves.
- Delivery: in person, online, hybrid; which LMS; accessibility requirements.
- Assessment plan and weighting; existing materials to reuse.
- Deadlines: approval dates, term start, when materials must be posted.

## Plugins that pair well (optional; suggest only if not installed and useful)
Productivity (task list, weekly prep) · Design (handouts and slides) · Operations (process docs for a program that repeats).

## What could go wrong (start the check with these)
Approval comes late · outcomes and assessments don't line up · materials aren't accessible · content not ready before the week it's taught · student data ends up in the wrong place.
