## How to change something
Tell Lead in plain words, e.g. **"change: no morning brief"**, **"change: call sessions 'desks'"**, **"change: Lead runs on Sonnet"**, **"change: put reports in Google Drive"**. Lead:
1. Says in one or two lines which files it will update.
2. After the owner's OK, updates them all together (handover, rules, briefs, cheat sheet, model guide, usage plan, scheduled tasks).
3. Logs it below. To undo, say "undo change <#>".
