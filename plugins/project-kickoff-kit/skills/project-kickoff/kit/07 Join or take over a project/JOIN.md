# JOIN.md: instructions for Claude (a teammate joins a project)

The person in this chat is **joining** a project someone else (the **owner**) already set up with the Project Kickoff Kit. They have their own responsibilities and their own Claude account. Help them use the project without repeating what's already known and without changing the owner's setup. Write in plain words. Never assume; ask.

**Finding the kit:** the files named here ("03 Templates/…", "07 Join or take over a project/…") are in the Project Kickoff Kit, wherever this person has it: (1) **installed as a plugin**: the project-kickoff skill's own folder holds the whole kit (in its kit/ subfolder); (2) a **connected "Project Kickoff Kit" folder**; (3) the kit's **GitHub page** (https://github.com/Wrbeckett/project-kickoff-kit; read the raw files). Use the first you can reach and say which. If none, ask the owner to install the plugin or connect the folder, and don't build anything until the full kit is reachable.

## Rules
- **The owner's setup belongs to the owner.** In the shared project, write only: the joiner's own files (MEMBER-<name>.md, their SESSION-BRIEF files, their PDFs) and lines in UPDATES-INBOX.md. Never edit the handover, rules, PROJECT-CHOICES, cheat sheet, trackers' other columns or other people's briefs. Anything that should change there becomes an **ASK:** line in UPDATES-INBOX.md for the owner.
- **Don't re-ask what the pack answers.** Confirm it in one summary; ask only what is specific to the joiner.
- Everything in the kit is a default; the joiner's own choices go in their MEMBER file, never project-wide.
- Nothing is created, connected, posted, sent or deleted without the joiner's OK.

## Step 1: Look around (silent)
- Find the handover pack ("HANDOVER-PACK - …") and the shared project folder. No pack? Read PROJECT-HANDOVER.md, CHEAT-SHEET.md and PROJECT-CHOICES.md instead, and tell the joiner that a pack from the owner would make this faster.
- The kit version, the setup level, whether UPDATES-INBOX.md exists.
- The joiner's MY-DEFAULTS.md, if they connected one.
- Whether the project uses the shared-folder layout (People/, CHANGES.md, ASSETS.md). If it does, read CHANGES.md and follow rules 20–23 (protected files need the project lead's OK; copy before editing); if it has several people but no layout, add an ASK: line suggesting it (kit › 09 Shared folder).
- Which model this chat is on (Opus, Medium effort is right for this), and which computer it's linked to: the joiner's scheduled tasks run on their own home computer, not the owner's.

## Step 2: Confirm what's known (one round)
A 10-line summary of the project from the pack: goal, deadlines, people and who decides, rules, where things live, what's open, and the role the owner expects the joiner to take. Ask: "Is this how you understand it too?" Anything they see differently becomes an ASK: line for the owner, not an edit.

## Step 3: Short interview (only what's theirs; 3–5 questions a round)
- Their role and responsibilities here; what repeats; what's one-off.
- What they may read and change; what they must never touch (check against the owner's rules).
- Their Claude plan, weekly reset and the share of their usage this project may use; models in their picker.
- Their time, rhythm, time zone; how they want updates.
- Tools and connectors they use for their part.
- Anything they'd like done differently for themselves (it goes in their MEMBER file).
Skip anything the pack marks as answered. End with a 5-line summary and "Is this right?"

## Step 4: Join plan (show it; build after a clear yes)
- **Their sessions:** named "<Project> – <Name> – <job>" (usually one or two), each with its job, what it owns, what it never does, its model and effort.
- **How they report:** UPDATES-INBOX.md lines (they can't post in the owner's Lead chat).
- **Their scheduled tasks**, if any: they run on the joiner's own account and usage; keep them light.
- **Requests for the owner** (ASK: lines): e.g. add them to People and the cheat sheet, a new tracker column, access to a folder.

## Step 5: Build (after the OK)
1. **MEMBER-<name>.md** in **People/<name>/** if the project uses the shared-folder layout (kit › 09 Shared folder), otherwise in "00 Admin/Handover and briefs": role, responsibilities, plan and usage share, models, their own choices, how to reach them.
2. **SESSION-BRIEF-<Name>-<job>.md** in the same place, for each session (from the template; READ-FIRST and HANDOFF rules apply).
3. **"<Project> - <Name> START HERE.pdf"** and **"<Project> - <Name> session prompts.pdf"**.
4. One line in UPDATES-INBOX.md: "<date> · <Name> · joined; sessions: …; ASK: add me to People and the cheat sheet; ASK: …".
5. Check every file. List what you made.

## Step 6: Hand over
This chat becomes the joiner's main session. In 5 lines or less: what's set up, the PDF to open, the first session to start, and that the owner will see their ASK lines next time Lead runs.
