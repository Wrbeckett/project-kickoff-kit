# CLOSE.md: instructions for Claude (closing a finished project)

The owner wants to wrap up a project, or pause it for a long time. Make sure nothing keeps running or using their Claude usage, everything is easy to find later, and what was learned isn't lost. Write in plain words. Never assume; ask.

**Finding the kit:** the files named here ("03 Templates/…", "07 Join or take over a project/…") are in the Project Kickoff Kit, wherever this person has it: (1) **installed as a plugin**: the project-kickoff skill's own folder holds the whole kit (in its kit/ subfolder); (2) a **connected "Project Kickoff Kit" folder**; (3) the kit's **GitHub page** (https://github.com/Wrbeckett/project-kickoff-kit; read the raw files). Use the first you can reach and say which. If none, ask the owner to install the plugin or connect the folder, and don't build anything until the full kit is reachable.

## Rules
- **Nothing is deleted.** Scheduled tasks are turned off, not deleted. Files move to Archive, they aren't removed.
- Every step is shown first and done after the owner's OK.
- Anyone else on the project (joiners, an adviser) is told by the owner, not by Claude. Offer a short draft note.

## Step 1: Ask (one round)
1. **Close or pause?** Close = finished. Pause = back later (keep everything as is, only turn runs off).
2. Anything still open that should be finished, handed to someone (a handover pack, Prompt 11) or dropped?
3. Keep the Claude Project and chats as they are (recommended), or archive them in the app?

## Step 2: Inventory (read only)
- Every scheduled task for this project: name, schedule, computer, last run.
- Open items in the handover, briefs, trackers and UPDATES-INBOX.md.
- Shared folders and who they're shared with; connectors only this project used.

## Step 3: Close-out plan (show it, then do it after the OK)
- **Turn off** each scheduled task (list them). Check each one shows as off.
- **Final summary:** "<Project> - Final summary.pdf" in "01 Briefs and reports": goal and how it went against the success measures, what was delivered (with where it lives), decisions that matter for the future, open items and who has them, and lessons.
- **Archive:** move work-in-progress and drafts to "Archive" (keep 03 Final, 04 Assets and 05 Reference where they are). List every move.
- **Handover:** mark PROJECT-HANDOVER.md "CLOSED <date>" (or "PAUSED <date>, restart with: …") at the top, with a pointer to the final summary.
- **Sharing:** list what's still shared; the owner decides what to unshare (they do it; give steps).
- **Lessons for next time:** with the owner's OK, add the useful ones to their own MY-DEFAULTS.md (if connected) (things they'd always do or skip) and anything about the kit itself to KIT-FEEDBACK.md (or tell the owner to pass it on).

## Step 4: Hand over
In 5 lines or less: what's turned off, where the final summary is, what's still shared, and how to restart (paste Prompt 9 or "restart this project" in the Lead chat). Pausing: say exactly what was left running (nothing, unless the owner chose otherwise).
