# Grow a project: step by step

Projects grow. Use this when you want to **add something new** to a project that's already set up, or **add, change or remove a person**. Claude asks a few questions, shows you a small plan, and changes only what's needed after your OK. About 5–15 minutes.

Do this in the project's **Lead** chat, on your home computer. Pick **Opus**, **Medium** effort.

## Add something new to the project
Type:

> add to this project: <your idea, e.g. "monthly newsletter to clients">

(or just **add to this project**, and Claude will ask.)

1. **Answer a few questions** (5 min): what it is, one-off or repeating, who does it, what it touches, when, and whether it brings in personal information.
2. **Claude says where it fits**, with the reason and the alternatives:
   - a job for a session you already have;
   - a new session;
   - a tracker (work passing between people or sessions);
   - a scheduled task (it repeats on its own);
   - or a **separate project**, if it's big enough to have its own goal and people.
3. **Claude says what it needs:** tools (plugins, connectors: always optional), the model to use, what it does to your weekly Claude usage, and what could go wrong.
4. **Approve the small plan** ("OK to add this?"). Claude updates the handover, briefs, cheat sheet and model guide, and tells you the first step.

## Add a collaborator
Type:

> add a collaborator: <name>

1. **Answer a few questions** (5–10 min): why you need them, their role, what they'll own, what they can only request, which folders they need, how they'll report, their Claude plan, and any privacy limits.
2. **Approve the plan.** Claude writes their **role card** (a draft they confirm later), updates the people list, owners table and cheat sheet, makes their own folder, and makes a **handover pack** with their role filled in (and a zip with a READ ME FIRST, if you want).
3. **You share the folders** (Claude gives you the exact steps; it never shares anything itself) and send them the pack.
4. **They type "join this project"** in their own Claude. Their Claude confirms the role card with them and sets up their part.

A second person usually means the **Team** setup level: Claude offers the shared-folder setup (owners, change log, backups) if it isn't there yet.

## Change someone's role
Type **change <name>'s role**. Claude shows what changes (duties, files they own, access, reporting) and updates everything together after your OK. You do any sharing or unsharing.

## Remove someone
Type **remove <name> from this project**. Claude lists everything they own and asks who takes over each item, lists the folders and Claude Projects to unshare (you do it), and the scheduled tasks in their account they should turn off. Their folder moves to Archive; nothing is deleted.
