# PEOPLE.md: instructions for Claude (add, change or remove a collaborator)

The owner (or project lead) wants to **add a collaborator** ("add a collaborator", "add <name> to this project"), **change someone's role** ("change <name>'s role") or **remove someone** ("remove <name> from this project"). Help them decide the role clearly, set up access safely, and keep the project's files in step. Write in plain words. Never assume; ask.

**Finding the kit:** see "Finding the kit" in README.md (plugin, connected kit folder, or the kit's GitHub page).

## Rules
- Only the owner or project lead runs this. The collaborator confirms their own part later in their own Claude (07 Join or take over a project/JOIN.md).
- **Claude never shares, unshares, invites or emails anyone.** It gives the owner the exact steps; the owner does it.
- Nothing is deleted: removed people's files move to Archive or get a new owner.
- Write about people respectfully and factually: the role card is something the collaborator will read. No private opinions about them.

## A. Add a collaborator

### Step 1: Interview (1–2 rounds; multiple choice where possible)
1. **Who and why:** name, and what the project needs from them.
2. **Their role:** what they'll do; what's one-off and what repeats; what "done well" looks like.
3. **What they own:** which shared files or work they'll own (the handover's owners table), and which they can only request changes to (protected files go through the project lead).
4. **Access:** which folders to share, view or edit; their own People/<name>/ folder (shared-folder projects).
5. **How they report:** UPDATES-INBOX.md lines; any reports or briefs they should receive.
6. **Their Claude:** do they have an account? Which plan they'll likely need (a paid plan for the kit plugin) and the model and effort for their kind of work.
7. **Privacy:** does their work involve personal information the project's rule 15 doesn't cover yet? If so, ask question bank §5b for it. Does their own organization add limits? (They confirm this in JOIN.)
End with a 5-line summary and "Is that right?"

### Step 2: The plan (show it; build after a clear yes)
- Their **role card** (draft of MEMBER-<name>.md, from 03 Templates/MEMBER-template.md).
- Changes to the handover's **People** section and **owners table**, the **cheat sheet**, and their People/<name>/ folder.
- **Setup level:** a second person usually means **Team**: offer the shared-folder set (09 Shared folder) and UPDATES-INBOX.md if they're missing.
- The **JOIN handover pack** (07 Join or take over a project/HANDOVER-PACK.md), with the role pre-filled so their interview is short; offer the zip with READ ME FIRST.
- **Sharing steps** for the owner (exact menus for their storage: Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive), view or edit per folder.

### Step 3: Build (after the OK)
Write the role card (marked "draft: to confirm with <name>"), update the files above, create their folder, make the pack (and zip, if wanted), then give the owner the sharing steps and what to send. Log it in PROJECT-CHOICES.md and, in shared-folder projects, CHANGES.md. Check every file.

## B. Change someone's role
1. Read their MEMBER file and what they own.
2. Ask what changes: duties, files they own, access, reporting, model.
3. Show the change (role card, owners table, cheat sheet, access steps: what to share or unshare).
4. After the OK: update all of them together, add a note to UPDATES-INBOX.md for the person ("<date> · Lead · your role changed: …"), and log it.

## C. Remove someone
1. List what they own (files, tracker rows, sessions, scheduled tasks), what's shared with them, and open items assigned to them.
2. Ask the owner: who takes over each item? Should their People/<name>/ folder move to Archive?
3. Show the plan; after the OK:
   - reassign every item (owners table, trackers, briefs);
   - list the **folders and Claude Projects to unshare** (the owner does it; exact steps);
   - list **their scheduled tasks** to turn off: they're in the person's own Claude account, so the owner asks them to turn them off (Claude can't);
   - move their folder to Archive; mark their MEMBER file "Left <date>"; update People and the cheat sheet; log it.
   Nothing is deleted.
