Join or take over a project: step by step

For the person receiving a handover pack: a teammate joining a project someone set up with the kit, or a new owner taking over a project someone started for them. You won't have to answer everything from scratch: Claude starts from the pack, checks it with you, and only asks what's missing. About 10 minutes of setup, then 10–30 minutes with Claude.

What you need

  • A Claude account and the Claude desktop app (Mac or Windows) on your home computer: the one that stays on, where you'll start the project's sessions and scheduled tasks. Turn off automatic sleep while it's plugged in and leave the app open (the kit's START HERE shows how on Mac and Windows).
  • The handover pack (a file named "HANDOVER-PACK - …") and any files sent with it.
  • The Project Kickoff Kit: the shared kit folder, or the kit installed as a plugin.

A. Joining a teammate's project

1. Get the shared folder (2 min)

Accept the shared project folder (Dropbox, Google Drive…) and let it sync. The pack is inside it.

2. Make your own Claude Project (2 min)

In the Claude app: Projects › New project, named " ()". Start a new chat inside it.

3. Connect and pick the model (1 min)

  • › Add folder › the shared project folder (and the kit folder, unless you use the plugin). Pick Opus, Medium effort.

4. Paste the prompt (1 min)

Prompt 12 from 04 Prompts to copy:

I'm joining . Read "07 Join or take over a project/JOIN.md" in the Project Kickoff Kit and the handover pack in the connected folder. Confirm what's already known with me, ask only what's specific to my role, and change nothing in the shared files except my own.

5. Confirm, answer, approve (10–20 min)

Claude sums up the project in 10 lines; say what's off. Then a few questions about your part: your role, your tasks, your Claude plan, how you like updates. Approve the plan; Claude builds your sessions and a START HERE PDF just for you.

Day to day: your updates and requests go in the project's UPDATES-INBOX.md (one line each); the owner's Lead session picks them up. You don't change the owner's files.

B. Taking over a project someone started for you

1. Make your project folder (2 min)

Make a folder named after the project (Dropbox, Google Drive, iCloud or your computer). Put the pack and any files that came with it inside.

2. Make a Claude Project (2 min)

Projects › New project, same name. Start a new chat inside it.

3. Connect and pick the model (1 min)

  • › Add folder › your project folder (and the kit folder, unless you use the plugin). Pick Opus, Medium effort.

4. Paste the prompt (1 min)

Prompt 13 from 04 Prompts to copy:

I'm taking over from . Read "07 Join or take over a project/TAKEOVER.md" in the Project Kickoff Kit and the handover pack in the connected folder. Go through the pack with me, ask only what's missing, then set the project up for me.

5. Go through the pack (10–20 min)

Claude shows what's in the pack, a few items at a time. For anything marked as someone's notes or a suggestion, choose keep, change or drop. Only your answers count as decisions. Then a few questions the pack couldn't answer (your Claude plan, your time, your rules).

6. Approve the blueprint, then Claude builds it

Same as a new project: folders, a handover, sessions, your START HERE PDF. Your first chat becomes your Lead session. If the person who started it is staying on as an adviser, your setup says how to reach them.
