Hand off a project: step by step

For the person who has the project's information. Use it when a teammate is joining a project you set up with the kit, or when someone is taking over a project you started for them (for example, a friend or client you've been helping). About 10 minutes of your time, plus a few minutes to review the pack.

1. Open the right chat (2 min)

  1. Open the Claude Project where the work lives. For a kit project, open its Lead chat. For a project you started without the kit, start a new chat inside that Claude Project, so Claude can read its docs and past chats.
  2. Connect the Project Kickoff Kit folder (+ › Add folder), unless you've installed the kit as a plugin. Connect the project folder too, if there is one.
  3. Pick Opus with Medium effort (click the model name next to the send button).

2. Paste the prompt (1 min)

Copy Prompt 11 from 04 Prompts to copy:

Read "07 Join or take over a project/HANDOVER-PACK.md" in the Project Kickoff Kit and make a handover pack for , who is this project. Read only; show me the pack before saving it.

3. Answer three questions (2 min)

  • Joining or taking over?
  • Who's receiving it and how they'll use Claude.
  • Your role afterwards: for a takeover, stay on as an adviser or hand it off fully.

4. Review the pack (5–10 min)

Claude labels every fact with where it came from: decided, confirmed by them, your notes, Claude's suggestion or verify. Their Claude will check the uncertain ones with them instead of treating them as decisions.

Claude leaves these out unless you say otherwise:

  • your own Claude plan and usage, and your other projects;
  • your private notes and opinions about the person (they will read the pack);
  • passwords, keys and account numbers ("exists; ask ");
  • personal information about other people.

Check the "left out" list, change anything, then say "save it".

5. Send it

  • Taking over: email the pack (and the "handover files" folder, if Claude made one) or put them in a folder you share with them.
  • Joining: share the project folder with them; the pack is inside it.

Then send them "Join or take over - step by step.pdf" from the kit's folder 07, or the kit's link if it's on GitHub.

6. Afterwards

  • Joining: their updates and requests arrive in the project's UPDATES-INBOX.md; your Lead session reads them.
  • Taking over as an adviser: their Claude knows to send history questions to you.
