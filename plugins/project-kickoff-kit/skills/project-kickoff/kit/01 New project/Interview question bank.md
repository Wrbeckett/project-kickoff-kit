# Interview question bank (for Claude; new projects and gaps in existing ones)

Skip anything the owner's MY-DEFAULTS.md already answers (confirm it in the summary). Pick only what the project needs. Ask 3–5 at a time, multiple choice where possible (your recommendation first, always allow "other"). Follow up on anything vague, any number that's missing, and any "it depends".

## 1. The project
- In one or two sentences, what is the project?
- Why are you doing it? What happens if it doesn't get done?
- Who is it for (customers, students, a client, yourself)?
- What does "done" look like? What does "success" look like, with a number if possible (sales, sign-ups, grade, launch date, pages)?
- Hard deadlines? Soft milestones?
- Is this a one-off, a pilot, or something that will keep running?

## 2. People
- Who's involved, and what does each person do?
- Who makes the final call on decisions? On spending? On anything public?
- Does anyone else need reports (team, client, manager, partner)? How often?
- Will other people use Claude on this project? Do they have their own accounts? Where will the shared project folder live (Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive), and who owns which shared files? (They'll give updates through a shared UPDATES-INBOX.md file, since only you can post in your Lead chat.)

## 3. The work
- What are the main kinds of work? (build/code, design, writing, video, marketing/social, sales, admin/finance, research, teaching, events, purchasing, shipping…)
- Which work repeats (daily, weekly, monthly)? Which is one-off?
- Which parts do you want Claude to do, which to help with, and which to stay out of?
- What usually goes wrong or gets forgotten on projects like this?

## 4. Where things live
- Where are the files now? (Dropbox, Google Drive, iCloud, OneDrive, computer, school/company drive)
- Which accounts and apps does the project use? (website host, store/payments, email, calendar, social, design, code repo, spreadsheets, AI tools)
- What may Claude read? What may it change? What must it never touch?
- Any existing docs, briefs, plans or past chats Claude should read first?

## 4b. Home computer (always ask if anything will be scheduled)
- Which computer will be this project's home computer: the one that stays on with the Claude app open? (A desktop is best; a laptop only if it stays plugged in and awake.) Mac or PC?
- Does it sleep on its own, or get shut down at night? OK to turn off sleep while plugged in?
- Do you also use Claude on another computer or your phone? (Fine for chatting; new sessions and scheduled tasks go on the home computer.)
- Are the project folders synced on the home computer (Dropbox, Google Drive, iCloud)?
- **Laptop (or a computer that's often off)?** When can it reliably stay open, plugged in and awake with Claude running: which days and times (the **work window**)? About how long?
- Reminder before each work window: phone notification (Claude mobile app installed and signed in), email, or both? How long before (15, 30, 60 minutes)? A "done, you can close it" message at the end?

## 5. Rules and limits
- Budget? Who approves spending?
- Things Claude must never do (e.g. send emails, post, delete files, deploy, spend money, use certain tools)?
- Brand, style, tone or accessibility rules? Legal, privacy, school or company policies?
- Anything confidential that must stay out of shared files?
- Will the project involve personal information about other people (students, clients, staff)? What does your organization's policy allow in shared files and AI tools?

## 6. Time and rhythm
- How many hours a week do you have for this? When (days, time of day)?
- Your time zone?
- Do you want a morning brief? Weekly reports? Which day and time?

## 6b. Claude plan and usage budget (always ask)
- Which Claude plan are you on (Free, Pro, Max 5x, Max 20x, Team, Enterprise)? Not sure? Settings › Usage shows it, with your weekly limit and when it resets. A screenshot is the easiest answer.
- When does your weekly usage reset (day and time)? When does the plan renew?
- What share of your weekly usage can this project use (e.g. 50 %, 75 %)? How much do you need to keep free for other projects and everyday use?
- Are other projects already running scheduled tasks or long chats on this account?
- Which days are your heavy Claude days, so big jobs and scheduled runs stay off them?
- Has your usage ever run out mid-week? What were you doing at the time?
- If this project gets close to its share mid-week, what should happen: pause the "nice to have" runs, ask you first, or keep going?
- Which models show when you click the model name next to the send button (Haiku, Sonnet, Opus, Fable)? Which do you usually leave selected? On Pro or a standard Team seat, Fable uses paid usage credits: use it when needed, ask first, or never?
- Any kinds of work where Claude has felt too slow or long-winded, or not smart enough? (This sets the model and effort for each session.)
- OK to send a usage screenshot once a week, so the weekly review can check the pace?

## 7. How you like to work
- Short updates or detail? Chat, PDF, or both?
- Words you prefer for things (e.g. "orders" vs "sales")?
- Anything that annoys you in how AI or tools give instructions?
- Do you want step-by-step instructions with exact menu names?
- Anything in the kit's usual setup you want to skip, rename or do differently? (It goes in PROJECT-CHOICES.md.)

## 8. Risks and unknowns
- What worries you most about this project?
- What don't you know yet that you'll need to find out?
- What would make you stop or change direction?

## Closing check (always)
Read back a 10-line summary: goal, success, deadline, people, work types, where things live, rules, rhythm, usage budget, models, tools, open questions. Ask: "Is this right? Anything to add or change?"
